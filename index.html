01 / WhyA note from the director

Journalists across the country describe changes in how they cover government: in access to records and officials, in the risks their sources face, and in pressure on their newsrooms. Many of us have talked about these shifts informally. What's missing is evidence. Are these one-offs? Two-offs or three-offs? Or the new normal? And if this marks a lasting change in how government works in the United States, what does it mean for how we do our jobs?

Surveying the news and information ecosystem is always a challenge. As Hub director, I'm asking for your help to make sure this survey reaches far, wide and deep. Please take it yourself, then forward it to reporters, editors, producers, photographers and freelancers who cover U.S. communities at the local, regional or state level.

The survey, Reporting Under Pressure: State of the Craft, From Washington to City Hall, takes about 5 minutes. Responses are confidential and will be reported only in aggregate. It closes on Monday, November 23, 2026. www.medill.school/state-of-the-craft

Deborah Douglas Director, Medill Solutions Journalism Hub, Northwestern University
